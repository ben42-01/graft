# Plans and limits

_What Free, Premium and Enterprise include, and what happens at a limit._

| | Free | Premium | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seats | 1 | 15 | Unlimited |
| Plugins enabled | 3 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Entities | 3 | 25 | Unlimited |
| Records | 2,000 | 100,000 | Unlimited (fair use) |
| File storage | 250 MB | 20 GB | 1 TB |
| Dashboards | 1 | 10 | Unlimited |
| Public forms live | 2 | 20 | Unlimited |
| Internal forms | 3 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Submissions per month | 100 | 5,000 | Unlimited (fair use) |
| API requests per minute | 60 | 600 | 6,000 |

## Trial

Every new workspace gets a **14-day Premium trial** with no card. When it ends, you move to Free.

## What happens at a limit

- **At it**: the action is refused with a message naming the limit. Submissions stop until the period resets.
- **A feature your plan lacks**: the screen explains the feature and what plan has it.

## Downgrading

Nothing is ever deleted. When a workspace drops to a lower plan, over-limit forms are unpublished (you choose which stay), and over-limit entities and records become read-only. Upgrade again and they come back.

## Upgrading

The owner upgrades under **Account** with Stripe Checkout, monthly or annually.
