# Operations

_Run the day: today, orders, customers, inbox, board and schedule._

**Operations** is where you work once the workspace is set up.

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Today** | What is starting today, what is out now, what is due back today and money owed |
| **Orders** | Every order, filterable by status. Open one to edit it, move it through its statuses, take payments and attach payment links |
| **Customers** | Everyone who has ordered, with spend and balance |
| **Inbox** | Every form submission, newest first |
| **Board** | Orders as a pipeline by status |
| **Schedule** | A timeline of bookings across your resources, with buffers shown |

## Overview

The **Overview** page shows headline numbers: open orders, what is owed, the last 30 days against the 30 before, and customers. A sales report adds a daily series, best sellers, which form orders came through and repeat customers. Free workspaces see the headline figures; the full sales report is Premium.

## Dashboards

**Dashboards** are layouts of widgets you arrange yourself:

| Widget | Shows |
|---|---|
| Record list | The latest rows of an entity |
| KPI | A number, such as usage against a plan limit |
| Calendar | A date field plotted |
| Chart | Usage over time (Premium) |

Widgets are bound to an entity, which is why entities come first. Free allows 1 dashboard, Premium 10, Enterprise unlimited.
