# Workspace templates

_Set a whole workspace up from a ready-made business template._

A workspace template is a blueprint for a type of business. Applying one creates everything the business needs in one go: entities, sample records, bookable pools and forms.

## How applying works

1. Open **Templates** and pick one.
2. Answer a few questions. Typical ones: can customers book online, do you take a deposit, what do you call your rooms, which optional modules do you want.
3. **Preview** shows exactly what would be created and what it costs against your plan's limits, before anything is written.
4. **Apply** builds it.

Only an owner or manager can apply a template, because it creates forms. If a plan limit would be exceeded, the preview says so up front.

## After applying

Everything a template creates is ordinary: rename entities, add fields, edit or delete forms. A field's key is permanent once saved, but its label is free to change.

## Entity templates

Separately, **Entities → Templates** offers single-entity starting points: Assets, Bookings, Customers, Deals, Employees, Expenses, Invoices, Jobs, Leads, Products, Projects, Suppliers, Tasks, Tickets and Time entries.
