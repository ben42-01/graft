# Overview

_Every public Graft endpoint, grouped by area._

102 endpoints, generated from Graft's API catalogue. Read [Conventions](../conventions/) first for the response envelope, errors, pagination and rate limits.

- [Authentication](./auth/) (7): Create an account, sign in and manage the session.
- [Current user](./me/) (1): Who you are and what your plan allows.
- [Entities and records](./entities/) (17): Define your own data objects, then read and write their records, including batch imports and images.
- [Forms](./forms/) (11): Build internal and public forms, publish them and manage their images.
- [Public forms](./public/) (4): The unauthenticated surface a visitor's browser uses: submit a form, browse its catalogue.
- [Submissions](./submissions/) (1): The inbox of everything visitors have sent through your forms.
- [Orders](./orders/) (11): Orders, their lifecycle, payments and payment links.
- [Customers](./customers/) (2): People who have ordered, derived from orders.
- [Invoices](./invoices/) (4): Invoices issued against orders.
- [Inventory](./inventory/) (10): Bookable pools, availability, holds and allocations.
- [Payments](./payments/) (3): Stripe Connect, so a workspace can take card payments.
- [Reports](./reports/) (3): Headline numbers, sales and usage.
- [Dashboards](./dashboards/) (5): Widget layouts for the overview screen.
- [Usage meters](./meters/) (1): How much of a plan limit has been used.
- [Plugins](./plugins/) (3): Switch workspace capabilities on and off.
- [Workspace templates](./workspace-templates/) (4): Set a whole workspace up from a ready-made business template.
- [Guided setup](./setup/) (3): The guided setup run for a new workspace.
- [Onboarding](./onboarding/) (2): The first-run wizard state.
- [Team](./team/) (5): Invite people and manage seats. Owner only.
- [Billing](./billing/) (1): Upgrade to Premium.
- [Media](./media/) (1): Read back images the workspace owns.
- [System](./system/) (3): Health, readiness and the public signing keys.
