# What is Graft?

_A workspace you shape to your business, without rigid CRM or ERP software._

Graft is a business management system for local and medium-sized businesses. Instead of forcing every business into the same fixed data model, each business gets a **workspace** it shapes itself: a barber shop, a plumbing company and a boat-hire firm all run on Graft and see completely different screens.

## The problem

Off-the-shelf tools each solve one slice, such as bookings, invoicing or contacts. Businesses end up juggling four to six subscriptions. Enterprise suites are too expensive and too complex. And every business is different, so a fixed data model never fits.

## The answer

Graft assumes almost nothing about what a "customer", "job" or "booking" is. You define it.

- **Entities** describe the things you track, with the fields you choose.
- **Records** are the actual data: one customer, one job, one boat.
- **Forms** collect records, internally for your team or publicly for your customers. A public form is a shareable link you can post anywhere.
- **Orders, payments and inventory** turn a form submission into a priced, scheduled, paid job.
- **Dashboards and reports** read it all back out.

## What you can do with it

- Take **bookings** for boats, rooms, equipment or your own time, with double-booking protection.
- Run a **shop** where customers pick items into a cart and you invoice or take payment.
- Capture **enquiries and quotes** from a form on your website or social media.
- Track **customers, orders and money owed** in one inbox.
- Invite your **team** with roles.

## Start quickly

You don't have to build from nothing. When you sign up, you can start from a **workspace template** for your kind of business, such as Graft Salon, Graft Hotel or Graft Trades, or follow the **guided setup** that asks a few questions and builds the pieces for you.
